# The Night of the Raster Beam: the script

> Approved as the way to go on 2026-11-23, made shot by shot and chapter by chapter. This is where the game is going. The game you can play now still follows the old story (see this file on the branch `dev`).
>
> Spoilers: the whole game, with its ending.

## In short

It is 1986. A boy types in a game from a magazine, all evening, line by line. It does not work and he does not know why. He has to go to bed and forgets to switch off the computer.

In his dream he is **Pixel**, a small light inside the machine. His own typing mistakes rain down on him, and something passes over the world that he does not understand: **the Beam**. He is scared. But in every place he finds out how one part of the machine works, and what he understands he can use. At 06:00 he stops running from the Beam and steers it. At 07:00 he wakes up, sits down and types. He knows this machine now, every nook and cranny.

The mistake was one character. The game he fixes is this one.

## Rules of the film

- Black bars above and below in the scenes. They open when you get control and close when you lose it.
- Few words, at most two lines at a time. They type themselves.
- Never a literal C64 screen. We see the glow of the monitor, not what is on it, until the last scene.
- Nobody dies. `LIVES` is `COURAGE`. `GAME OVER` is `THE NIGHT STARTS OVER`.
- The clock is always on screen. It is how far you are from morning.
- One new thing per chapter. Every power is learned in the same three steps: it **scares** you, you **see how it works**, you **use** it.
- Fire skips every scene.

## Intro (about 30 seconds)

**Shot 1. Black.** Only the hum of a monitor. A blue glow comes up slowly and shows a room in silhouette: a desk, a bed, a window with stars. A small figure sits in front of the light. Keys tick.

> He typed it in. Every line.

**Shot 2. The same room.** The ticking stops. One flat, wrong tone. The figure does not move.

> It does not work.
> He does not know why.

**Shot 3. Light falls in from a door.** A long shadow across the floor.

> "Bed. Now."

The door closes. The figure is in the bed. The monitor still glows.

**Shot 4. The alarm clock, large: 22:59.** The black bars close slowly, like eyelids. The glow is the last thing left, a thin line. The clock jumps to **23:00**, and the line of light opens into a deep, slow field of stars. A small warm light lies in the middle. It opens its eye: Pixel.

> It is 23:00.
> The computer is still on.

> Hold on until morning.

**Shot 5. The title.** The line of light passes from top to bottom and draws the letters `RASTER RUMBLE` as it goes. This is the Beam. Nobody knows that yet. Then: `PRESS FIRE`.

## 23:00 The Screen

- **Feeling:** wonder.
- **Card:** `23:00` / *So this is what it looks like inside.*
- **Picture:** deep blue, slow clouds of light. Pixel is the only warm thing.
- **Sound:** the calmest version of the tune.
- **Play:** the bugs fall, one at a time, and you learn to fly. Fire gives a spark. It does nothing.
- **The Beam:** every ten bugs a soft line of light passes over the world. It is harmless. Everything it touches is bright for a moment.
- **End of the hour:** a bug passes close by. It has the shape of a character.
  > He knows that one. He typed it.
- **Courage gone:** *He pulled the blanket over his head.*

## 01:00 The Clocks (CIA)

- **Feeling:** something is off.
- **Card:** `01:00` / *Something is counting.*
- **Picture:** the clouds move in steps, like the hand of a clock. Short faults. Blue turns to green.
- **Sound:** a tick under the tune.
- **It scares:** the Glitch. It hangs there, slow, and suddenly rushes.
- **He sees it:** the Glitch does not rush at random. It ticks, always the same number of times, down to zero. Who listens, knows.
  > It is only counting down.
- **He uses it: SLOW.** Fire slows the world for a short time, not Pixel. Every near miss winds the clock up again.
- **The Beam:** it passes faster, and it leaves the dark behind it a little longer.
- **End of the hour:** all ticking stops at once. Silence.
- **Courage gone:** *The clock jumped back.*

## 03:00 The Sound (SID)

- **Feeling:** fear.
- **Card:** `03:00` / *He hears it before he sees it.*
- **Picture:** the edges close in and the colours fall away. Dark purple and grey. You only see what is close.
- **Sound:** the tune breaks into its three voices. Each kind of enemy has one.
- **It scares:** bugs that drift in from the side, out of the dark. First the voice, then the bug.
- **He sees it:** a loud note makes a ring in the dark, and the bugs bend around it.
  > Sound is a wave. A wave can push.
- **He uses it: WAVE.** Fire sends a ring out from Pixel. It pushes back what is near and lights it up.
- **The Beam:** for the first time it stops. Halfway down the screen, as if it is looking. Then it goes on.
- **End of the hour:** a wave goes out and does not come back. There is no wall out there.
- **Courage gone:** *It was so loud that he heard nothing.*

## 04:30 The Memory (RAM)

- **Feeling:** alone.
- **Card:** `04:30` / *Nothing here. Or everything.*
- **Picture:** almost black. Long corridors that glide by at different speeds. Only Pixel gives light, and it is small.
- **Sound:** the hum and one thin voice.
- **It scares:** the powers are gone, and fire gives only the spark again. Pale copies of every bug come back, two at a time.
- **He sees it:** the corridors are not empty. Here and there something glows: a tick, a note. Things he learned tonight, still where he left them.
  > Nothing is lost. You have to know where it is.
- **He uses it: REMEMBER.** Fly through a glow and that power is back. From now on fire uses the powers in turn, and the top row shows which one comes next.
- **The Beam:** not here. That is worse.
- **End of the hour:** far away a line of light. It is coming this way.
- **Courage gone:** *He forgot where he was.*

## 06:00 The Beam (VIC)

- **Feeling:** courage.
- **Card:** `06:00` / *There it is.*
- **Picture:** black. The Beam is the only light, white and very bright. Every time it passes, a strip of the world is drawn behind it.
- **Sound:** everything together, the tune at full strength.
- **It scares:** the Beam sweeps from top to bottom with one gap, faster every time. Pixel has to be in the gap. SLOW gives time to reach it, WAVE clears the bugs on the way.
- **He sees it:** the last sweep has no gap. There is nowhere to go. It touches Pixel, and Pixel is still there, brighter.
  > It was never hunting him.
  > It was drawing him.
- **He uses it: STEER.** Hold fire and the Beam follows Pixel up and down. Each band of the dark that you sweep becomes morning: first red, then orange, then blue sky. The bugs that the light touches turn back into characters and fall into place, in rows.
- **End of the hour:** the whole picture is drawn. The Beam goes thin and quiet, as it was on the title.
- **Courage gone:** *Not yet. Once more.*

## 07:00 The Room

- **Feeling:** awake.
- **Card:** none. The alarm clock, large: `07:00`. It rings. The black bars open like eyelids.
- **Picture:** the room of the intro, but now in morning light. You see its colours for the first time. A bird outside.
- **Scene:** the figure sits up and looks at the monitor, still on. He walks to it and sits down.
- **Play:** every press of fire is a key. The sounds he learned are in the keys: a tick, a note. The glow on his face changes with each one.
  > He sees it now.
  > One character.
- **The last key:** a long second of nothing. Then the line of light passes over the little monitor in the room and draws, very small, `RASTER RUMBLE`.
  > It works.
- **The disk:** we move into the monitor until it fills the screen. `A GAME BY` and three places for your initials. The drive spins: your name and score go to the disk.
- **Last words,** over the sound of keys:
  > Now he can make anything.
- Black. The hum. A click: the monitor is off. `PRESS FIRE` to play another night.
